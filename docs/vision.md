# PTO website vision statement

PTO is a website that enables a small community of friends and family to play a
fun and friendly football predictions game.

Each Premier League gameweek, players predict upcoming matches. As the gameweek
progresses, they view how accurate each prediction was and compare their points
to others on the leaderboard. Players track their overall standings across the
season.

The website is also used for summer football tournaments, such as a World Cup.
These games raise money for charity, which is handled outside of the PTO
website.

Matches, kickoff times and final scores are updated automatically by the backend
for the most part, but organisers can override this using the website if needed.
